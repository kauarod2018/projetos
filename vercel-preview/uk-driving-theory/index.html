Frequently asked questions

Anyone preparing for the car theory test in Great Britain, especially if your test is coming up soon, you learn better with visuals, or you need to revise quickly after a fail.

This is version 0.1.0. Each map is checked against The Highway Code and official GOV.UK guidance, and the date of the check is printed on every page (12 Oct 2026). When the rules or the test change, we update the PDFs and you get the new version free.

Is this official DVSA material?

No. It's an independent revision guide based on The Highway Code and official public guidance. All the practice questions are our own, not taken from the official question bank.

Does it cover Northern Ireland?

It's designed for the theory test in Great Britain (England, Scotland and Wales). Where the rules differ between England, Scotland and Wales, the maps say so.

How do I receive it?

Right after you pay, Hotmart emails you your access to the members area, where you download the PDFs.

Can I print it?

Yes. Every map is an A4 page, made to print in colour or black and white.

Does it work on my phone or tablet?

Yes, the PDFs open on any device. They're designed for A4, so they're most comfortable on a tablet or printed; on a phone, zoom in. The flashcards also come in a phone version.

Does it cover hazard perception?

Yes, the Complete pack includes a visual guide to how hazards are scored and what to look for. You'll still need to practise with video clips.

Are the files editable?

No. They're high-resolution PDFs for your personal use.
